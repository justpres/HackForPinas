Tambayan chat gives you a lightweight way to meet other participants without leaving the directory.

## Lobby chat

The lobby is the shared community room. Use it to ask broad questions, find teammates, or share useful event updates.

### Good lobby etiquette

- Keep messages helpful and relevant to the community.
- Avoid posting sensitive personal information.
- Link to official sources when you share deadline or application updates.

## Direct messages

Select a participant from the chat sidebar to start a private conversation. Direct messages are useful for following up on a teammate search or coordinating around a specific event.

## Reporting an issue

If a message seems unsafe, misleading, or spammy, do not amplify it. Capture the relevant context and contact the project maintainers through the repository.
