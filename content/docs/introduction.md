Tambayan is the home for people building, learning, and competing in the Philippine hackathon community. It brings active opportunities and helpful context into one place.

## What you can do here

- Browse hackathons from universities, public agencies, and private organizers.
- Filter opportunities by status and keep an eye on approaching deadlines.
- Join the lobby chat to ask questions and meet other builders.

## Finding your first event

Start on the [events page](/) and open an event card that matches your interests. Each event page includes the organizer, timing, eligibility, and an application link when one is available.

### Keep your details current

Event details can change quickly. Always confirm final requirements and submission dates through the organizer's official link before applying.

## A community-first directory

The directory is public and community-driven. If you find an opportunity that should be listed, use the submission flow to share it with the review team.
